About this QR code generator

This is a free, open-source QR code generator that runs entirely in your browser. There is no account, no upload and no tracking: the text you type is encoded on your device, so your URLs, Wi-Fi credentials and contact details never leave your machine. Unlike many online generators, the codes are plain static QR codes — they do not expire, redirect or route through a third-party service.

Supported content types include website URLs, plain text, Wi-Fi network access, vCards, email, SMS and phone numbers. You can customise the foreground and background colours, change the module shape, raise the error-correction level, add a centre logo, and export a high-resolution PNG or a vector SVG — each with the quiet zone the QR specification requires.

Frequently asked questions

Are these QR codes free and permanent?

Yes. The generator is free and every code it produces is a standard static QR code. Static codes never expire, never redirect through a third party and never require a subscription.

Does the generator store or transmit my data?

No. Everything is encoded in your browser. There is no backend, no upload and no analytics, so the URL, Wi-Fi password or contact details you enter never leave your device.

Can I add my own logo to the QR code?

Yes. Upload a PNG, JPEG, WebP or SVG file up to 2 MB. Error correction is raised automatically and the logo size is capped at a value verified to still decode.

What export formats are supported?

You can download the result as a PNG between 512 and 4000 pixels or as a vector SVG. Every export includes the four-module quiet zone the QR specification requires.

Can I generate a Wi-Fi QR code?

Yes. Enter the network name (SSID), password and security type (WPA, WEP or none). Special characters are escaped so they cannot break the payload.
